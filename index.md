# About {-}

> This workbook contains the practical exercises for **LECX4101: Foundation Skills for LEC**.

These exercises will help you learn the skills that you will need to complete both pieces of coursework for the module. 

## Learning objectives for the practicals {-}

After completing all ten practical exercises, you should be able to:

1. Use a variety of software tools to explore data; describe and think critically about patterns in the data.
2. Use Excel to perform calculations, make figures, and prepare data for analysis in other software.
3. Use R and RStudio to wrangle data, calculate descriptive statistics, and create a variety of figures (e.g. box plots, histograms, bar plots, scatter plots, and hexbin plots).
4. Use ArcGIS to examine spatial data and create a map.
5. Explain the importance of conducting data analyses in a transparent and reproducible manner.

## Questions, cautions, warnings, and tips {-}

Periodically the practical instructions will be interrupted by a blue question box like the one below. Please take a few minutes to consider the question inside, either on your own or by chatting with people around you.

This will help you get the most of the practical exercises and is the best way to prepare for your coursework.

::: {.rmdquestion}
***Question:*** What pattern do you expect to see in this data? What might be causing this pattern?
:::

Other times you will see an amber or red box with a warning to either pay attention to or avoid something.

::: {.rmdwarning}
***Warning!*** Be careful about how you do this thing.
:::

::: {.rmddanger}
***Danger!*** Do NOT do this terrible thing.
:::

Finally, sometimes you will see green boxes with helpful tips or other important information.

::: {.rmdtip}
***Tip!*** Here is a helpful tip or some useful information. 
:::
