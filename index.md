# About {-}

> This workbook contains the practical exercises for **LECX4101: Foundation Skills for LEC**.

These exercises will help you learn the skills that you will need to complete both pieces of coursework for the module. 

## Module learning objectives {-}

After completing all ten practical exercises, you should be able to:

1. Use Excel to perform basic calculations and prepare data for analysis in other software.
2. Use R and RStudio to wrangle data, calculate descriptive statistics, and create a variety of figures types (e.g.boxplots, histograms, bar plots, and scatter plots).
3. Explain why data analyses should be transparent and reproducible whenever possible.
4. Use ArcGIS to examine spatial data and create a map.

## Questions, cautions, warnings, and tips {-}

Periodically the instructions will be interrupted by a blue question box like the one below. Please take a few minutes to consider the question inside, either on your own or by chatting with people around you.

This will help you get the most of the practical exercises and is the best way to prepare for your coursework.

::: {.rmdtip}
***Question:*** What pattern do you think might appear in this dataset? What might be causing this pattern?
:::

Other times you will see a yellow or red box with a warning to either pay extra-careful attention to or avoid something.

::: {.rmdwarning}
***Attention!*** Be careful about how you do this thing.
:::

::: {.rmdcaution}
***Warning!*** Do NOT do this terrible thing.
:::

Finally, sometimes you will see green boxes with helpful tips or other important information.

::: {.rmdimportant}
***Important!*** Here is a helpful tip. 
:::
